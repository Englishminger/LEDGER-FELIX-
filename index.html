Ledger

Reads invoices, refunds and recurring bills out of your Gmail. Nothing leaves your device — it talks to Google directly from your phone.

1

Create a Google OAuth Client ID

In Google Cloud Console → Credentials → Create Credentials → OAuth client ID → Web application. Add this page's exact URL under "Authorized JavaScript origins". Full steps are in README.md.

2

Paste your Client ID

Your Client ID is stored only in this browser. You'll approve an "unverified app" warning the first time you sign in — that's expected for a personal-use app that hasn't gone through Google's public review.

Settings

A web app can only sync while it's open on screen — it can't wake itself up in the background the way a native app can. Opening Ledger triggers a sync automatically; the interval above just keeps it fresh while you're looking at it.
